# Privacy Policy — NYC Buses On A Glance

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

NYC Buses On A Glance ("the app") is an open-source Wear OS app that shows nearby New York City
bus arrivals. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

## Location
The app uses your watch's location only while you are using it, to find bus stops near you.
Your coordinates are sent directly from your watch to the MTA Bus Time API
(`bustime.mta.info`) as part of the request for nearby stops. The developer never receives,
stores or sees your location. The MTA's handling of those requests is covered by the MTA's
own privacy policy.

## Data stored on your watch
The app keeps these on the watch only: your starred buses, your settings (search radius and,
if you enter one, your MTA API key), and the last results so the app opens quickly. They are
not backed up or sent anywhere, and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Sharing
The developer does not collect, sell or share any personal data. The only network requests
the app makes are to the MTA Bus Time API.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
